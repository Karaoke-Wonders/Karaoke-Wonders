Our Mission

Karaoke Wonders was created with one simple goal: to give the VRChat community a place where people can come together, discover music, and peacefully sing the songs they love. But Karaoke Wonders is more than just a VRChat world. It is a community and a platform built specifically around that experience. From our VRChat world and song library to our website, Discord, account systems, moderation tools, and community services, every part of Karaoke Wonders is designed to work together as one ecosystem. Can't find the song you want to sing? Request it through our website. Want to take part in the community? Join our events and Discord. Need help? Our support and moderation teams are here to assist. We believe the best experience comes from having control over the systems we depend on. That's why our website was built from scratch specifically for Karaoke Wonders, without relying on pre-existing libraries or components. Our goal is to build the tools our community needs ourselves, allowing us to shape the experience around our users rather than forcing our community into someone else's platform. Karaoke Wonders began with a vision and continues to grow through the people who build, support, and participate in it. Whether you're here to sing, listen, meet people, request music, contribute to the community, or simply have somewhere to relax, you're welcome here. Sing what you want. Meet who you want. Make Karaoke Wonders your place.
