### I want to be a caretaker, how do I apply?

### I don't see a song I want to sing in the world, how do I add them?
You can request songs in https://karaokewonders.com/main

### Who is in the KW (karaoke Wonders) Team?
You can see everyone on the KW team through https://karaokewonders.com/about.

The KW Team consists of Developers for Karaoke Wonders. They should not be messaged for any reason.

To join the KW team, you can apply to be a developer through https://karaokewonders.com/careers.

If you don't see a position you see fit but think they are useful for Karaoke Wonders, notify a caretaker so they can take it up. If you don't see any positions in the website, it means we aren't looking for developers.

### How often will songs be approved?
They will be approved mainly every day but also depends on the availability of the senior caretakers or above that have access to approve/reject songs.
 
It also depends on how many songs for that day there is.

### What do I do if I want a song I made removed from the world?
You can create a ticket and request a removal of the song after proving that you are the original creator of that song.

### What if we need a song edited?
If you accidentally mistyped a sing and need it edited, create a ticket to request an edit.
