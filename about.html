About Karaoke Wonders

VRChat Karaoke World

Our Mission

Karaoke Wonders was created to allow the users of VRChat to go to a place to peacefully sing the songs they want and if they don't see a song they like, they are able to request in the main website! (Also the website was made from scratch with no pre-existing libraries or components)

To Karaoke Wonders:

Free music

True Karaoke world that only holds instrumentals to allow you to sing over it.

Cross-Platform

Fully optimized for both VRChat PC and Quest/mobile users.

Active Community

Community events every so often to get together and have some fun.

Staff & Safety

If you feel a caretaker has mistreated you or abused their authority, you have the ability to report them to a Manager of Caretakers.
